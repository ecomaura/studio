Year / Footprint

Architectural Statement

An elemental dialogue between alpine granite and cedar warmth.

Perched high on a steep rocky incline facing the Engadin valley, the project was envisioned as a geometric stone emergence rather than an intrusion into the landscape.

By cantilevering the main living pavilion toward the southwest, internal spaces capture sunlight from dawn to sunset. The interior palette is rigorously restricted to three authentic ingredients: raw hand-poured board-formed concrete, reclaimed Swiss larch, and dark brushed bronze fittings.

Studio Profile

Atelier Vaucluse

An independent architectural and interior studio committed to architectural permanence, tactile honesty, and elevated living.

The Atelier

Rigorous craftsmanship.

Operating as a bespoke design laboratory, Atelier Vaucluse rejects industrialized speed in favor of obsessive structural detail and spatial balance.

Our multidisciplinary teams of architects, interior architects, and material researchers craft spaces where light and raw substance evoke visceral stillness. We serve clients who value nuance, provenance, and enduring design integrity.

Methodology

The 4-Stage Creative Process

Phase 01

Topography & Immersion

In-depth site investigation, solar orientation, spatial flow study, and client lifestyle distillation.

Phase 02

Schematic Geometry

Translating programmatic needs into volumetric plans, physical clay models, and spatial axis lines.

Phase 03

Material Synthesis

Direct quarry visits, tactile mockups, custom joinery prototyping, and technical MEP integration.

Phase 04

Realization

Meticulous on-site execution oversight ensuring every junction, shadow reveal, and stone vein meets atelier standards.
